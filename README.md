# Growth Audit — SmartyMe

**Live:** https://sofiapikuleva.github.io/smartyme-flow-rebuild/

Four ranked ideas for Micro Learning - SmartyMe, plus a clickable prototype of the membership card, both paywalls and the gift, rebuilt in SmartyMe's own design. Prepared by Adapty, October 2026, from the live iOS flow and the App Store listing.

## The four ideas

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Make the surprise a real gift | Gift on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Name their topic on the paywall | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Bring your proof to the first paywall | Paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 04 | Hand over the membership card before the price | Membership card | CR +10–20% |

Every screen in the prototype is SmartyMe's own design. Only the sequence changed, plus a few things SmartyMe already has but does not show at the right moment.

## Grounding

Every price, rating, review and claim comes from SmartyMe's own published material: the US App Store listing (4.5 from 59K ratings; Annual Subscription USD 59.99, Monthly Subscription USD 14.99, Annual Subscription Promo Offer USD 29.99) and the in-app screens (Join 4M+, Trustpilot Excellent, What you get, 200+ courses, the App Store and Trustpilot reviews, the membership card).

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for SmartyMe.

## Run locally

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open http://localhost:8000. Add `?open=proto` to open straight on the prototype.
