What this is

We opened SmartyMe the way a new learner does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what actually moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at SmartyMe: installed it, went through the first paywall, the membership card and the surprise that follows, read your App Store listing, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you currently show people.

What follows is four ideas — not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours — your membership card, your “Choose your plan” paywall with its green plan cards and SAVE badge, your gift pop-up and your green paywall with “What you get”, the 4M+ laurels and Trustpilot. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already have but do not show at the right moment.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us — we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
